- Pet details lock once a booking is confirmed, so the pet that arrives is the pet that was booked.

## 4. Bookings

1. An owner requests a stay from a host; both sides agree the **terms** in chat: dates, pet, where the pet stays, the price (paid or volunteer) and whether food is provided.
2. A booking is **confirmed** when both sides accept the terms. There is no booking fee.
3. When the booking is confirmed the exact location and contact details are unlocked for the two of you.
4. On **handover day** both sides take a photo of the pet in the app. Do the same at pick-up.
5. Either side can cancel a booking in the app. PawsPal does not hold or move any money for a stay, so there is nothing to refund through PawsPal. Repeated no-shows or last-minute cancellations can lead to enforcement under section 10.
