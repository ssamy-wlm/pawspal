# Community Guidelines

PawsPal works because neighbours trust each other with the animals they love. Every member agrees to these five rules.

## The five rules

### 1. Care for every pet like your own
Food, water, walks, love and quick updates. Follow the owner's instructions. If something is wrong, tell the owner straight away and get a vet if needed.

### 2. Use real photos only
Your own face as your profile photo, and your own pets. No filters, sunglasses, group shots, cartoons or stolen images.

### 3. Keep chat and payments in the app until a booking is confirmed
Don't ask for or share phone numbers, WhatsApp, Instagram, InstaPay, bank details or links before then. The app hides them automatically, and repeated attempts are a rule break.

### 4. Be honest and kind
In messages, listings and reviews. Describe your pet and your home truthfully. No harassment, threats, insults or discrimination.

### 5. Report problems
Use **Report** on any profile, chat or booking. Every report is read by a person. Reporting in good faith never counts against you.

## How we enforce them

| Step | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Reminder** | A first, minor slip (for example a blurry face photo) | A friendly note explaining the rule |
| **Warning** | A clear break of a rule | Recorded on your account; you are told which rule |
| **Suspension** | Repeated or serious breaks | Your profile is hidden for 3 days, 7 days or **until fixed**. You see exactly what to fix |
| **Removal** | Danger to a person or animal, fraud, fake identity, or breaking the rules again after suspension | The account is closed and the person cannot rejoin |

You can **appeal** any warning, suspension or removal in the app. A person reviews every appeal and replies.

## Zero tolerance

These lead to immediate removal and, where appropriate, a report to the authorities:

- harming, neglecting or abandoning an animal
- threats or violence against a person
- fake identity or someone else's ID
- selling, breeding or fighting animals through PawsPal
- fraud or stealing payments

## Safety tips

- Meet in daylight for the first handover, and take the handover photo together.
- Check the pet's vaccination record and vet contact before the stay.
- Keep all messages in the app; it protects you if something goes wrong.
- Use **Safety Center › Emergency** if a pet is ill, injured or lost.
